# Guided Labs Catalog

The academy contains 80 project-specific guided hands-on labs.

## Project 01: Lending Operations & Underwriting App

**Industry:** Financial Services

| Week | Guided lab | Time | Steps | Research topic |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Guided Lab: Establish the product and development workspace | 4 to 6 hours | 8 | How Salesforce multitenancy and metadata-driven architecture affect solution design. |
| 2 | Guided Lab: Build the application data model | 5 to 7 hours | 8 | Lookup versus master-detail relationships and their effects on ownership, deletion, sharing, reporting, and roll-ups. |
| 3 | Guided Lab: Add data quality and guided user entry | 4 to 6 hours | 8 | How data-quality controls should be divided among validation rules, duplicate rules, Flow, and Apex. |
| 4 | Guided Lab: Configure and test least-privilege access | 5 to 7 hours | 8 | How Salesforce object, field, and record security layers work together. |
| 5 | Guided Lab: Automate the core business process with Flow | 6 to 8 hours | 8 | Flow versus Apex and how to choose the correct automation layer. |
| 6 | Guided Lab: Build the Apex service layer | 6 to 8 hours | 8 | Object-oriented programming in Apex and why service boundaries matter. |
| 7 | Guided Lab: Create efficient SOQL, SOSL, and data-access utilities | 5 to 7 hours | 8 | Efficient SOQL design, selective queries, and large-data-volume considerations. |
| 8 | Guided Lab: Implement bulk-safe trigger orchestration | 6 to 8 hours | 8 | Trigger architecture patterns, handler classes, service layers, and recursion prevention. |
| 9 | Guided Lab: Harden Apex security | 5 to 7 hours | 8 | User mode, system mode, sharing, CRUD, and field-level security in Apex. |
| 10 | Guided Lab: Create the automated test framework | 6 to 9 hours | 8 | What makes an Apex test valuable beyond code coverage. |
| 11 | Guided Lab: Integrate the instructor-provided external service | 7 to 10 hours | 8 | Queueable Apex versus Batch Apex versus Platform Events. |
| 12 | Guided Lab: Build the primary Lightning Web Component workspace | 7 to 10 hours | 8 | How Lightning Web Components use modern web standards. |
| 13 | Guided Lab: Add advanced LWC, search, files, and performance | 7 to 10 hours | 8 | Lightning Data Service versus custom Apex and how performance and accessibility affect component design. |
| 14 | Guided Lab: Run the Git, quality, deployment, and release workflow | 6 to 8 hours | 8 | Git workflows, continuous integration, release validation, and structured root-cause analysis. |
| 15 | Guided Lab: Use AI, MCP, CLI, and reusable agent skills safely | 6 to 9 hours | 8 | Benefits and risks of AI-generated Salesforce code, plus MCP servers versus APIs and Salesforce CLI. |
| 16 | Guided Lab: Complete, test, and demonstrate the Agentforce-enabled application | 10 to 14 hours | 8 | Designing secure and reliable Salesforce agents with grounding, guardrails, monitoring, and escalation. |
